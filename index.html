About Us

Harish Exporters Service Ltd

We specialize in providing reliable and efficient export and logistics solutions. With a strong commitment to quality, speed, and client satisfaction, we help businesses connect with global markets seamlessly. From handling documentation to coordinating international deliveries, our experienced team ensures your goods reach their destination safely and on time. Whether you're a small business or a large enterprise, we tailor our services to fit your unique export needs.

Our Services

Perishable Cargo

We specialize in transporting temperature-sensitive goods, ensuring freshness, quality, and timely delivery through controlled environments and expert handling for fruits, vegetables, flowers, seafood, and other delicate perishables.

Live Animals

We provide safe, humane, and regulated transport for live animals, ensuring comfort, compliance with international standards, and expert care throughout the journey for pets, livestock, and wildlife species.

Warehousing

Our secure, spacious warehouses offer inventory management, climate control, and real-time tracking, ensuring safe storage, easy accessibility, and efficient distribution for a wide range of goods and products.

Air and Sea Freight Services

We offer reliable air and sea freight solutions with global reach, competitive rates, timely delivery, and seamless coordination for both small parcels and large-scale cargo shipments worldwide.

Clearing and forwarding

We handle customs clearance and documentation efficiently, ensuring swift cargo movement, regulatory compliance, and end-to-end logistics support for smooth import and export processes across international borders.

Inland Transportation

We provide reliable inland transport services using a modern fleet, ensuring timely delivery, cargo safety, and efficient connectivity between ports, airports, warehouses, and final destinations across the country.
